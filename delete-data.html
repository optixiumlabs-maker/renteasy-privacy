# How to Delete Your RentLog Data **App:** RentLog by Optixium Labs **Contact:** optixiumlabs@gmail.com ## Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended) 1. Open RentLog 2. Tap **Settings** (bottom tab) 3. Tap **Delete account & data** 4. Confirm both prompts ("Continue" then "Delete account") This will: - Delete all property, payment, repair and category records from this device - Delete every RentLog backup file from your Google Drive (if Drive backup was enabled) - Sign you out of Google - Cancel all scheduled rent reminders The deletion is **immediate and permanent**. No data is retained. ## Option 2 — Already uninstalled the app If you no longer have RentLog installed and want to remove data that was uploaded to your Google Drive: 1. Open https://drive.google.com on a computer or phone 2. In the search bar, search: `RentLog Backups` 3. Right-click the folder named **RentLog Backups** → **Move to trash** 4. Empty Drive trash You may also revoke the app's access: 1. Go to https://myaccount.google.com/permissions 2. Find **RentLog** → **Remove access** ## Option 3 — Email request If you cannot do the above, email **optixiumlabs@gmail.com** with subject "RentLog — data deletion request" and your Google account email. We do not store any user data on our servers, so this is only to confirm Drive cleanup. ## What data is deleted - All properties, tenants, payments, repairs, categories — stored only on your device - All backup files in your Google Drive (.json files in "RentLog Backups" folder) ## What data is retained - **None on our side** — RentLog does not have servers; we have no user database. - Google Drive's trash retains deleted items for 30 days before permanent deletion (Google's own policy, outside our control).
