Privacy Policy

RentLog by Optixium Labs

App package: com.optixiumlabs.renteasy

Contact: optixiumlabs@gmail.com

Last updated: November 2026

Short version: RentLog stores all your data on your own device. We don't run any servers, we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use any analytics SDKs. The only data that ever leaves your device is what you explicitly choose to back up to your own Google Drive.

1. What data we process

The following data is processed by the app:

  • Property information you enter — name, address, monthly rent.
  • Tenant information you enter — name, phone number.
  • Rent records — months, amounts, paid/pending status, paid dates.
  • Repair records — category, description, cost, date, contractor, status, notes.
  • Repair categories — labels and icons you create or use.
  • Google account name and email address — only if you enable Google Sign-In for Drive backup.

2. Where the data is stored

  • All data is stored locally on your device in an SQLite database managed by the app.
  • The app does not send data to Optixium Labs servers — we operate no servers for RentLog.
  • If you enable Google Drive backup, JSON backup files are uploaded only to your own Google Drive account, into a folder named "RentLog Backups".
  • The app does not share data with any third party.

3. Security

  • All communication with Google services (Sign-In, Drive) goes over HTTPS / TLS.
  • The app requests only the drive.file scope, which limits access to files created by the app itself. We have no read or write access to any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Local data is stored in the app's private storage directory, isolated from other apps.

4. Google Sign-In (optional)

  • We use Google's standard OAuth 2.0 protocol.
  • We never see, store or process your Google password.
  • You can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

5. Account and data deletion

You can delete your data at any time using one of the methods described in our Data Deletion Instructions:

  • Settings → Delete account & data — wipes the local database, all Drive backups, and signs you out of Google.
  • Settings → Clear all data — wipes only the local database, keeps your Google sign-in.
  • Uninstall the app — Android automatically deletes all local data.

6. Advertising, analytics, tracking

  • The app does not show advertisements.
  • The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or any other analytics SDK.
  • The app does not use cookies, device fingerprinting, or any kind of tracker.
  • The app does not collect or use Android Advertising ID.
  • We do not profile users.

7. In-app purchases

RentLog offers an optional Premium subscription via Google Play Billing. Transaction processing is handled entirely by Google Play; we receive only the subscription status (active/inactive) for your account. We do not receive or store payment card details.

8. Permissions explained

The app does not request location, contacts, SMS, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

9. Children

RentLog is intended for users 18 years and older. The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly process personal information from children under the age of consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the date of the most recent change. Significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

For any privacy questions, email optixiumlabs@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 business days.
