RentEasy Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Data Collection

RentEasy stores data ONLY locally on your device. We do not collect, send, or sell personal data to third parties.

Google Account

For Google Sign-In we use the standard OAuth protocol. We have no access to your password. Backup data is stored in your personal Google Drive account. You can revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com.

Subscription

RentEasy offers a free plan and Premium subscription (€4.99/month or €39.99/year). Subscriptions renew automatically via Google Play. Cancel anytime from Google Play → Subscriptions.

Data Deletion

Delete all data from Settings → Clear Data. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
